About LitTraceAI

LitTraceAI stands for Literature Traceability using Artificial Intelligence. It generates structured literature reviews from a predefined collection of research papers and lets researchers inspect the evidence behind the generated text.

Lit: Literature
Organises research papers into a structured literature review.
Trace: Traceability
Links each generated sentence to its supporting source sentences through citations and provenance identifiers.
AI: Artificial Intelligence
Uses AI models to analyse, compare and synthesise research, and to evaluate how source information is transformed and whether citations support the generated text.
